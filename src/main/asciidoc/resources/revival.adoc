[[poster]]
== Poster

The poster of the spex the revival revives, read from Spexregister.
`204 No Content` when the spex has no poster, and `503 Service Unavailable` while Spexregister is down.

operation::revival-poster-get[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-headers,http-request,response-headers']

[[swish_qr]]
== Swish QR code

The Swish QR code for a participant's unpaid fee, as an SVG.
`404 Not Found` unless the caller is a participant of the revival, it is `OPEN` and has a Swish number, and they owe an unpaid fee; `503 Service Unavailable` when Swish cannot be reached.

operation::revival-swish-qr-get[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-headers,http-request,response-headers']
